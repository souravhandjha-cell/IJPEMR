About IJPEMR

The International Journal of Pharmaceutical, Engineering and Multidisciplinary Research (IJPEMR) is an emerging peer-reviewed, open-access academic journal established to encourage the publication and exchange of scientific and scholarly research across pharmaceutical, engineering, scientific, technological and multidisciplinary disciplines.

The journal has been developed with pharmaceutical sciences as its central academic focus, while recognizing the growing importance of interdisciplinary research in solving complex scientific, technological and healthcare challenges.

IJPEMR provides an academic platform for researchers, faculty members, scientists, healthcare professionals, engineers, postgraduate students and other scholars to communicate research findings and scientific perspectives.

Mission

To promote ethical, transparent and high-quality scholarly communication by providing researchers with an accessible platform for sharing scientific knowledge and encouraging research that contributes to pharmaceutical sciences, healthcare, engineering, technology and related disciplines.

Vision

To develop IJPEMR as a credible scholarly platform that encourages research excellence, interdisciplinary collaboration and responsible scientific communication.

Core Values

Academic Integrity

Honesty and integrity throughout research and publication.

Scientific Quality

Scientifically sound and methodologically appropriate research.

Transparency

Clear authorship, funding and conflict-of-interest disclosure.

Ethical Publishing

Responsible editorial and publication practices.

Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Meaningful research across disciplinary boundaries.

Accessibility

Open access to scholarly content.

IJPEMR
