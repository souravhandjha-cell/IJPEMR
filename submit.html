Submit Manuscript

Authors may submit manuscripts to IJPEMR through the official editorial email address. Please prepare the manuscript according to the Author Guidelines and include complete author and affiliation details.

Online Manuscript Submission

Authors can submit their manuscript online by completing the form below. The manuscript file can be attached directly with the submission.

Submit Online Submit by Email

File limit: Total uploaded file size must not exceed 10 MB. PDF or DOC/DOCX is recommended.

Online Submission Form

On first use, the form service may send an activation/confirmation email to the journal's official email address. Complete that activation before accepting live submissions.

Submission Checklist

  • Manuscript prepared according to the journal guidelines.
  • All authors and affiliations listed correctly.
  • Corresponding author and contact email clearly identified.
  • Figures and tables properly labelled.
  • Ethics and informed-consent information provided where applicable.
  • Funding and conflict-of-interest statements included.
  • Manuscript is not simultaneously submitted to another journal.

After Submission

Submitted manuscripts are subject to initial editorial assessment. Suitable manuscripts may proceed to peer review. Authors should retain their manuscript ID and use the Track Manuscript page for status enquiries when a tracking record has been provided.

Current charges: Submission fee ₹0 and APC ₹0 currently. Any future change in charges will be communicated transparently through the journal website.
