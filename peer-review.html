Peer Review Policy

IJPEMR uses editorial assessment followed, where appropriate, by independent expert peer review.

Initial Screening

Submissions are checked for scope, completeness, originality concerns, ethical requirements and basic scientific quality.

Reviewer Selection

Suitable manuscripts may be assigned to reviewers with relevant subject expertise.

Review Model

A double-anonymized review process may be used where practicable.

Editorial Decision

Possible outcomes include acceptance, revision, or rejection. The final decision is made by the Editor-in-Chief or designated editor.

Confidentiality & Conflicts

Reviewers should maintain confidentiality, provide objective assessments and disclose conflicts of interest.
