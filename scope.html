Aim & Scope

Aim

The primary aim of IJPEMR is to provide a scholarly platform for dissemination of original, scientifically meaningful and ethically conducted research. The journal particularly focuses on pharmaceutical sciences while also welcoming high-quality research from engineering, science, technology, healthcare and other multidisciplinary areas.

IJPEMR encourages research that contributes to scientific understanding, technological advancement, innovation, healthcare improvement and interdisciplinary problem-solving.
