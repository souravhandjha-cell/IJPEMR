Advancing Research Across Pharmaceutical, Engineering, Scientific and Multidisciplinary Domains

International Journal of Pharmaceutical, Engineering and Multidisciplinary Research (IJPEMR) is a scholarly, peer-reviewed and open-access journal committed to the publication and dissemination of high-quality research in pharmaceutical sciences, engineering, science, technology, healthcare and multidisciplinary fields.

Pharmaceutical sciences constitute a core focus of IJPEMR. The journal also welcomes meaningful interdisciplinary research that demonstrates originality, scientific rigor, methodological soundness and contribution to existing knowledge.

About IJPEMR

IJPEMR provides researchers, academicians, scientists, healthcare professionals, engineers and students with a transparent and ethical platform for communicating research findings and scientific ideas. The journal encourages both fundamental and applied research with clear scientific or practical relevance.

Peer Review

Manuscripts undergo editorial screening and, where suitable, evaluation by independent subject experts. A double-anonymized process may be used where practicable.

Open Access

Published scholarly content is intended to be accessible to readers without subscription barriers.

Quarterly Publication

IJPEMR plans to publish four issues per year, subject to the journal's editorial and publication schedule.

Pharmacy Core

Strong emphasis is placed on pharmaceutics, pharmaceutical technology, drug delivery, pharmacology and related pharmaceutical disciplines.

Peer Review & Editorial Standards

IJPEMR follows a structured editorial and peer-review process designed to maintain the quality, integrity and scientific relevance of published research.

  1. Submission: Manuscript is submitted through the designated journal channel.
  2. Initial Editorial Screening: Scope, basic scientific quality, originality, ethics and completeness are assessed.
  3. Peer Review: Suitable manuscripts may be evaluated by independent reviewers with relevant subject expertise.
  4. Revision: Authors may be invited to respond to reviewer/editorial comments and revise the manuscript.
  5. Editorial Decision: The final decision is made by the Editor-in-Chief or designated editor based on the manuscript and review process.
  6. Publication: Accepted manuscripts undergo final editorial checks before publication.
