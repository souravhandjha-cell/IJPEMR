Author Guidelines

Authors should prepare manuscripts clearly, accurately and in accordance with responsible research and publication practices. Submission indicates that the manuscript is intended for consideration by IJPEMR and that the authors accept the journal's editorial and peer-review procedures.

General Formatting

  • Language: English.
  • Paper size: A4.
  • Margins: approximately 1 inch on all sides.
  • Font: Times New Roman, 12 pt for main text.
  • Line spacing: 1.5.
  • Number pages consecutively.
  • Tables and figures should be numbered and cited in the text.

Recommended Manuscript Structure

  1. Title
  2. Authors and affiliations
  3. Abstract
  4. Keywords
  5. Introduction
  6. Materials and Methods / Methodology
  7. Results
  8. Discussion
  9. Conclusion
  10. Acknowledgements
  11. Funding statement
  12. Conflict of interest statement
  13. Ethics statement, where applicable
  14. References

Originality & Ethics

Authors must ensure originality and appropriate citation of sources. Fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, duplicate submission and other forms of misconduct are not acceptable. Authors remain responsible for the accuracy and integrity of the work.

References

References should be complete, accurate and consistently formatted. Authors should verify bibliographic details before submission.
