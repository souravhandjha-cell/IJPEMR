Before Submission

Authors should prepare their manuscript according to the current Author Guidelines.

  • The manuscript should be original.
  • The manuscript should not be simultaneously under consideration by another journal.
  • Authors should provide accurate author, affiliation and corresponding-author information.
  • All sources and previous work should be appropriately cited.
