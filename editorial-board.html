Role of the Editorial Board

The Editorial Board supports the journal's scholarly publication process through editorial coordination, peer-review facilitation, manuscript assessment, publication-quality oversight, and support for ethical and transparent scholarly communication.

Editorial Standards

The journal aims to maintain a transparent editorial process, appropriate peer review, responsible publication practices, and clear communication with authors and reviewers.
